## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a synthesis of timeless Indian rural craftsmanship and contemporary digital clarity. The aesthetic balances the earthy permanence of Maharashtra’s artisanal traditions with the frictionless utility needed by rural micro-entrepreneurs, self-help groups (SHGs), and conscious urban consumers.

### Visual Character
- **Editorial Artisanal Precision:** Rooted in the rich cultural heritage of rural Maharashtra—Paithani silks, Warli narratives, brass metalwork, and agro-handicrafts—avoiding cold, sterile SaaS clichés without descending into faux-rustic pastiche.
- **Dignified & Human-Centered:** Celebrates producer identity, village provenance, and collective enterprise with pride, clarity, and structural balance.
- **Warm & Trustworthy:** Anchored by deep forest greens, sunbaked terracotta, unbleached cotton creams, and soft, natural shadows that evoke organic materials, clay pottery, and morning harvest light.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from the Western Ghats and Deccan Plateau: deep forest canopies, terracotta earthenware, sun-cured grain, and riverbed silt. High contrast ensures readability in outdoor sunlight on mobile displays.

### Role Tokens & Hex Codes
- **Primary (`#234F3D`):** Deep Forest Green. Used for primary CTAs, key brand signatures, heavy display headings, and navigation foundations.
- **Secondary (`#C96F4A`):** Sunbaked Terracotta. Used for action highlights, badge highlights, active indicators, and conversion accents.
- **Tertiary (`#16382B`):** Deep Night Forest. Used for maximum-contrast text, dark footer backgrounds, and structural framing.
- **Neutral Surface Canvas (`#F7F1E3`):** Raw Unbleached Cream. Serves as the warm base canvas, replacing clinical white with a paper-like tactile ground.
- **Surface Elevated (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white used strictly for foreground cards, floating sheets, and modals to lift elements above the neutral ground.
- **Surface Container Soft (`#DDE8DF`):** Pale Sage Green. Applied to input fills, tag backgrounds, table headers, and secondary badge backdrops.
- **Border Subtle (`#E5DAC6`):** Warm clay outline for cards, dividers, and input borders.
- **Success (`#2D6A4F`), Warning (`#D97706`), Error (`#B91C1C`):** Calibrated for accessibility against cream and white surfaces.

## Typography

Typography pairs the humanistic, stately elegance of **Playfair Display** with the utilitarian precision of **Inter**. 

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Playfair Display** is reserved strictly for display banners, section heroes, craft category headers, narrative quotes, and producer story titles. It commands attention and lends cultural reverence to rural enterprise.
- **Inter** handles high-density data, enterprise transaction tables, micro-copy, numeric metrics, forms, and functional navigation elements. It guarantees total legibility across varying mobile screens and outdoor lighting environments.
- Maintain a minimum line-height of 1.45 on mobile reading blocks to support multilingual scanning and high-density vernacular translations.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an **8pt modular baseline grid** with a responsive fluid layout. 

### Breakpoints & Grid Composition
- **Mobile (0 – 599px):** 4-column fluid layout with `16px` margins and `16px` gutters. UI structures stack vertically; CTAs span full width for reliable thumb ergonomics.
- **Tablet (600 – 1023px):** 8-column fluid layout with `32px` margins and `20px` gutters. Product showcases and producer grids transition into 2-up configurations.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid capped at a maximum container width of `1280px`, with `80px` outer margins and `24px` gutters.

### Rhythmic Discipline
- Spacing between related text elements (e.g., heading to subtitle) adheres to `0.5rem` or `0.75rem`.
- Spacing between disparate components (e.g., cards, metric blocks) scales from `1.5rem` on mobile up to `3rem` on desktop.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than synthetic grey shadows, depth is achieved through **warm earth-tinted ambient shadows** layered over physical-feeling substrate planes.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Raw Unbleached Cream (`#F7F1E3`) without shadows.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Product Tiles):** Solid white surface with a `1px` stroke in Warm Clay (`#E5DAC6`) paired with an ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -1px rgba(35, 79, 61, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(22, 56, 43, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Hovered Product Cards):** Elevates slightly on hover: `0 8px 20px -3px rgba(35, 79, 61, 0.10), 0 3px 6px -2px rgba(201, 111, 74, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Artisanal Header & Floating Action Bars):** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(22, 56, 43, 0.12)` with a subtle bottom border (`#E5DAC6`).
- **Level 4 (Modals & Verification Drawers):** `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(22, 56, 43, 0.22)` with backdrop blur filter of `8px` tinted at `rgba(22, 56, 43, 0.40)`.

## Shapes

With a roundedness index of **2**, elements carry deliberate curvature: standard components sit at `0.5rem` (`8px`), larger cards at `1rem` (`16px`), and hero containers at `1.5rem` (`24px`).

### Shape Rules
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Standard Buttons):** Formed with `0.5rem` (`8px`) radii, providing a grounded touch target without excessive roundness.
- **Craft Product Cards & Media:** Use `1rem` (`16px`) corners with hidden overflow to maintain soft editorial framing.
- **Badges, Provenance Chips & Metric Pills:** Use full pill shapes (`9999px`) to create clear visual contrast against structural rectangular content containers.
- **Subtle Cultural Geometries:** Architectural tabs, divider lines, and hero accent frames incorporate soft beveled terminal points inspired by traditional Maharashtrian wada wood carvings.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Controls
- **Primary Action Button:** Solid Deep Forest Green (`#234F3D`) fill, `#FFFFFF` text in Inter SemiBold (`14px`), `0.5rem` border radius, `12px 24px` padding. Hover transition shifts background to `#16382B` with a subtle Terracotta glow shadow.
- **Secondary / Craft Action Button:** Terracotta (`#C96F4A`) fill with `#FFFFFF` text for immediate regional conversion actions (e.g., "Contact Producer", "Buy Harvest").
- **Outline / Editorial Button:** Warm Cream background with a `1.5px` border in `#234F3D`, text in `#234F3D`. Hover shifts to `#DDE8DF` fill.

### Producer Provenance & Craft Product Cards
- **Structure:** Crisp white background framed by a `1px` solid `#E5DAC6` border, `1rem` corner radius.
- **Image Aspect Ratio:** 4:3 or 1:1 ratio with natural warm balance.
- **Provenance Header:** Overlaid pill tag containing a location icon, village name, and district (e.g., "Paithan, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar") using Pale Sage (`#DDE8DF`) background and `#16382B` text.
- **Artisan Identity:** Displays producer thumbnail, name, and cooperative/SHG affiliation in Inter Medium `12px`.
- **Pricing & Action:** Prominent currency indicator in Deep Forest Green, alongside an immediate order or inquiry CTA.

### Chips & Provenance Badges
- **Village Origin Chip:** Pale Sage (`#DDE8DF`) fill, `#16382B` text, `9999px` pill radius, `4px 12px` padding.
- **Terracotta Highlight Chip:** Semi-transparent Terracotta tint (`rgba(201, 111, 74, 0.12)`) fill, `#C96F4A` text, used for "Direct from Weaver", "GI-Tagged", or "Organic Certified".

### Inputs & Form Controls
- **Text Inputs:** `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF7F0` surface fill, `1px` border in `#E5DAC6`, `0.5rem` radius, `12px 16px` padding. Focus states transition border to `#234F3D` with a `3px` focus ring in `rgba(35, 79, 61, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom check controls with `#234F3D` active state and `#FFFFFF` glyph; inactive state features a `1.5px` border in `#C96F4A`.

### Metric Counters & Impact Badges
- **Impact Container:** Soft cream card with `#16382B` Playfair Display numbers (`36px`), accompanied by uppercase Inter labels (`11px`, tracking `0.04em`) in Terracotta (`#C96F4A`).
- **SHG Verification Shield:** Subtle heraldic seal element rendered in Deep Green and Terracotta to signify authenticity and direct fair-trade compliance.
